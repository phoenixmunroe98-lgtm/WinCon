    Your Rival

    Synthesized from the full Champions roster — not just what you've obtained — specifically to give your current team its hardest possible matchup. Uses the same greedy pick logic as Generate Dream Team, run in reverse: instead of picking your best 6 against a reference list, it picks 6 that best answer YOUR team's typing and stats.

    Toughest matchups

    Threats where none of your six currently has a clearly favorable answer.

    Every threat, member by member

    How this is scored: for each reference Pokémon, every member of your team is checked both ways — how hard the threat's likely STAB hits them (type effectiveness), how hard their own moves (or, if a slot's moves aren't filled in yet, their own types) hit back, and a rough Speed comparison using their Stat Points and Nature. Your team's answer to a threat is whichever of your six comes out best. This is not a simulated battle and not a measured win rate — the 16 reference Pokémon are a hand-picked list, not live usage data, and the Stat Point → stat-value math is the standard formula with SP treated as roughly EV/8, which the community dataset itself flags as still unverified.

    Team type coverage

    How your six hold up defensively against all 18 attacking types — independent of the reference threat list above. Use this to spot a shared weakness before it costs you a game.
