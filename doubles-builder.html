Simulated Win Rate

Simulating hundreds of battles against the Worlds-grounded reference field… this can take a few seconds.

How this is simulated: WinCon automatically picks your best bring-4-of-6 lineup — the real tournament team-preview rule — then plays out hundreds of full mechanical battles per opponent: real turn order (priority, then Speed, then a coin-flip tie), the standard damage formula with STAB, type effectiveness, critical hits and a random damage roll, stat-stage boosts and drops, and the curated ability/item effects and move secondary effects WinCon has mechanical data for — against a reference field grounded in real Pokémon Champions World Championships 2026 results plus known competitive archetypes (see data/meta-baseline.json). Every opponent battled is still one of those hand-verified teams — nothing here gets invented — but a team whose real members are currently winning a lot in live Regulation M-B tournaments gets sampled somewhat more often, so the reported win rate leans toward what's actually being played right now. In-battle move choices are a competent greedy AI, not perfect play, and only the Pokémon/abilities/items/moves WinCon has curated mechanical data for get their special effects modeled — everything else still battles on its base stats, typing, and raw move power, so treat this as a strong estimate, not a lab-perfect one. If your team has one or two Mega-Evolution-eligible members, each is simulated as its own separate scenario below, since only one Mega can actually be active in a real battle.

Your Rival

Synthesized from the full Champions roster — not just what you've obtained — specifically to give your current team its hardest possible matchup. Uses the same greedy pick logic as Generate Dream Team, run in reverse: instead of picking your best 6 against a reference list, it picks 6 that best answer YOUR team's typing and stats — with a real nudge toward whatever's actually winning in live Regulation M-B tournaments right now, same as Auto-build gets. Unlike Simulated Win Rate above, this stays a fast explainable heuristic (Matchup Score's own type/Speed comparison), not a full battle simulation — see its own note below.
