Battle Tracker

Log each real game's result for any of your saved teams — win or loss, which of your six you actually brought that game, an optional note (e.g. "vs Trick Room"), and whatever you remember of the opponent's team. Every logged result also adds to WinCon's shared, anonymized usage stats across every player — over time, that real data supplements Generate Dream Team, Auto-build team, and Auto-build strategy on the Builder pages, alongside the existing curated matchup data and the Simulated Win Rate reference field further up this page's own Team vs Team matchup table. See the note at the bottom of this page for how sparse that will be at first.
